The watch

OpenCV finds what moved. YOLO11s names the crop. One frame a second, on a Raspberry Pi 5 in Los Angeles. That reading is the work. The picture around it is the show.

  1. OpenCVMOG2 finds the motion on the mountain.
  2. YOLO11sA small network names the crop: car, walker, bike, truck.
  3. YouTubeThe named frame is rebuilt and pushed live.
